# Changelog

## v0.9.0-preview, November 2026

- Initial public preview of *MPC, Gently*.
- Eight-lesson beginner course for the Akai MPC Sample, written for firmware 1.3.
- One edition, 160 × 244 mm, sized for the iPad mini (61 pages).
- Lesson 1 introduces the instrument; Lessons 2 to 8 build one original track of about 80 seconds at 84 BPM.
- Illustrated control diagrams, teaching graphics, quick reference, troubleshooting and glossary.
- Clickable contents and PDF bookmarks.
- Checked against Akai's official MPC Sample User Guide; pending physical-hardware validation.
