## The handbook (CC BY-SA 4.0)

The original writing, illustrations, diagrams and page designs are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

If you adapt the handbook (for a newer firmware, a translation, a different page size):

1. Credit it as *MPC, Gently* by slowphase, with a link to this repository.
2. Say that you changed it.
3. Release your adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.
4. Keep the third-party credits in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md), remove or replace the Akai artwork unless you have permission to use it, and do not present your version as official Akai documentation.

## Akai artwork and trademarks

The handbook reproduces:

- the **Akai Professional** and **MPC Sample** logos, traced from Akai's official logo artwork (cover, lesson openers and the files in [`source/book/assets/`](source/book/assets/NOTICE.md)), and
- the **labelled top-panel diagram** from the official *MPC Sample User Guide* (page 5, and `source/book/assets/overview.png`).

These are **not** covered by CC BY-SA 4.0 or MIT. All rights in them remain with Akai Professional / inMusic. They appear here, with credit, so that beginners can recognise their instrument and find their way around it. The handbook preview images in `previews/` show some of them; those parts of the previews are likewise excluded.

"Akai Professional", "MPC" and "MPC Sample" are trademarks of inMusic Brands, Inc. They are used only to describe the hardware. This is an independent educational publication, not affiliated with or endorsed by Akai Professional or inMusic.

If you are a rights holder and would like an image changed or removed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/slowphase/mpc-gently/issues) and it will be dealt with promptly.
