# Credits

## The instrument this book is about

- **[Akai Professional](https://www.akaipro.com/)** and **inMusic**, for the [MPC Sample](https://www.akaipro.com/mpc-sample): a small, standalone sampler that makes a lovely first instrument.
- The official **[MPC Sample User Guide](https://www.akaipro.com/guides/mpc-sample/introduction.htm)** (v1.0, online and PDF editions), including its Tutorial, Features, Sample Mode, Pad Play, Sample Record, Sequence Mode, Song Mode, Effects and Menus chapters. Every button, menu and step in the handbook was checked against it.
- Akai's published notes on MPC Sample **firmware 1.2 and 1.3**.
